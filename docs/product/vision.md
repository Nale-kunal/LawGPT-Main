# Product Vision

## Mission
Empower Indian legal professionals with modern, affordable, and secure practice management technology that matches international standards.

## Target Users
- Solo advocates and small law firms in India
- Legal professionals managing 10-500+ cases
- Lawyers who need court hearing management, client tracking, and invoicing

## Competitive Advantages
- Purpose-built for Indian legal system (courts, acts, case laws)
- AI-powered semantic legal search
- Razorpay-native INR billing
- Modern web technologies (not legacy Java/PHP)
- Mobile-first responsive design
